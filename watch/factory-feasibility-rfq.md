# Starlink Watch — Factory Feasibility & Prototype RFQ

**Purpose:** Request a feasibility review and itemized quote for engineering and prototype work. This document does not authorize tooling, production, or purchase.

**Status:** For preliminary factory discussion. The governing documents remain the [vision](starlink-watch-vision.md) and [technical specification](technical-specs.md), revision 1.0 draft. The factory should flag conflicts, unknowns, and recommended changes rather than silently substituting materials or construction.

**Visual references:** [hero render](../renders/starlink-watch-hero.png), [open dial](../renders/starlink-watch-open-dial.png), [caseback](../renders/starlink-watch-caseback.png). Renders communicate appearance only; dimensions and materials are governed by the technical specification.

## Product brief

A hand-wound mechanical wristwatch with an Apple Watch-proportioned rounded-rectangle squircle case. The movement is visible through a partial front dial aperture and a full sapphire exhibition caseback. The target envelope is 45 × 38 × 12 mm, with 22 mm strap attachment, three hands, no date, and a preliminary 3 ATM water-resistance target.

The watch should use cool silver-family materials and finishes. Quote 925 sterling silver and 316L stainless steel as separate case options. For sterling silver, also quote the proposed hybrid construction with steel thread reinforcement at the caseback and crown.

## Requested response

Please respond with the following, separating confirmed capabilities from estimates and assumptions.

1. **Capability and DFM review**
   - Relevant experience making mechanical watches with custom rectangular or squircle cases, partial open dials, and exhibition backs.
   - Whether your team can engineer and supply the continuous-curvature case profile, custom squircle sapphire crystal, movement casing ring, partial dial, and square-case gasket system.
   - Which work is in-house and which requires a named subcontractor or movement supplier.
   - A marked-up list of conflicts, missing inputs, risks, and design changes you recommend before prototype.

2. **Movement and visible finishing**
   - Available hand-wound movement references, actual diameter/thickness, current availability, lead time, MOQ, and unit price at sample and production quantities.
   - Quote a Seagull ST36-family option first, and identify any ETA/Unitas alternative separately.
   - Feasibility, added cost, and lead time for decorative bridge finishing and selective skeletonizing in the front aperture zone. Confirm which bridges remain visible from front and back for the selected caliber.
   - Confirm whether the target case thickness remains feasible with the selected movement, crystal, dial, casing ring, and caseback stack.

3. **Itemized preliminary costs**
   - Engineering / DFM fee.
   - CAD and drawing preparation, identifying deliverable file formats and ownership/use rights.
   - Tooling and non-recurring engineering, itemized by case, crystal, dial, and any other custom component.
   - Prototype/sample unit price and sample quantity options.
   - Sample lead time and production lead time estimate.
   - MOQ and unit-price breaks at quantities you recommend quoting (for example 50 / 100 / 300), clearly marked as estimates.
   - Testing costs, including water resistance, case/dimensional inspection, and movement functional checks.
   - Packaging, shipping, payment terms, quote validity, and any taxes or fees excluded from the quote.

4. **Prototype plan**
   - Recommend a low-cost proof-of-concept sequence that validates movement fit, front aperture visibility, winding/crown position, case thickness, and caseback view before committing to production tooling.
   - Identify which parts can be mocked up or made with existing components and which require custom tooling for a meaningful prototype.
   - State the approval gates before any paid tooling or production work begins.

## Preliminary design targets to review

These are targets from the technical specification, not permission to proceed without confirmation:

| Water resistance | Preliminary 3 ATM target | Confirm architecture and actual validation method; not a swim watch |
| Case materials | 925 sterling silver or 316L stainless | Quote separately; include hybrid silver case with steel wear-point inserts |
| Movement finishing | Visible decorated bridges; selective openworking at front aperture | Confirm supplier capability, visual sample, cost, and lead time |

## Quote assumptions and exclusions

- Do not begin production tooling, purchase components, or manufacture saleable units based on this inquiry.
- Quote feasibility/engineering, samples, tooling, testing, and production separately.
- Do not substitute a standard rounded-square case for the requested continuous-curvature profile without identifying the deviation and showing a drawing/render for approval.
- Do not claim a water-resistance rating until the proposed construction has been tested and the test method and results are supplied.
- Please identify all assumptions and deviations in writing and return drawings or marked-up images with your response.
